Privacy

Privacy on this website

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version

This website does not have accounts, does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and does not store what you type into the contact form.

The contact form

The form works inside your own browser. When you press a button, it opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message already written, and you choose whether to send it. Nothing is sent to an Indrix server.

Once you send a message, we receive it through WhatsApp or email, and those services handle it under their own privacy policies. We use what you send only to reply to you and to work on your request. We do not sell or share it.

What is saved on your device

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser so the site remembers it next time. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it from your browser settings.

Links to other websites

This site links to our products and to other websites such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn and GitHub. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Questions or removal requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to nandaniramoliya@gmail.com.
